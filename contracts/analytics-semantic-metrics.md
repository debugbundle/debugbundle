# Semantic analytics calculation contract

Status: local calculation kernels and executable fixtures, a project ordered-funnel projection with conservative default-disabled API/CLI/MCP query, and internal source-bound portfolio projection/read. The readers exclude facts after identity authority is lost and expose an `excluded_events` count. Portfolio results remain separate by source, with explicit catalog gaps and no cross-project distinct total. Source coverage is unverified and quality partial; public space reports, connected reports, broader metrics and reporting UI remain open. This is part of the unfinished contract-freeze gate, not a claim that semantic reports are available.

## Ordered funnels

`packages/analytics-engine` owns pure calculation and finite predicate compilation. This implements the calculation portion of FR-ANL-37 and AC-ANL-SEMANTIC-METRICS. Existing V1 funnel aggregates remain unchanged.

The compiler consumes a protected, admitted event and an activated definition under a currently authorized scope snapshot. It never authenticates a client or grants access. The caller must validate catalog references and prospective effective dates, verify all source permissions, apply exclusions consistently, and retain the admission policy generations before compilation. A space definition also requires the complete current source-catalog revision set, each covered source's event-entry revision and compatible same-name semantics, with explicit partial source coverage as specified in `analytics-semantic-control.md`; omitting an inaccessible or undeclared source cannot silently change the denominator. Session subjects are framed with their origin project. Other subjects are framed with their explicitly authorized identity scope, subject kind and namespace revision. Account membership comes from the occurrence's admitted account reference. A user reference cannot stand in for an account, anonymous reference or session. Matching hashes from different project namespaces remain different subjects; a connected space requires a matching space identity namespace.

The retained calculation input is a compact, closed fact: event and origin IDs, protected content hash, opaque resolved subject key, subject unit, scope/definition revisions, occurrence/receipt time, producer stream/sequence, at most ten predicate-match booleans and a bounded entry-breakdown candidate. It contains no raw event body or subject export. A first project-only worker projection now stores these facts inside durable job completion and prunes them within detailed history. An internal owner/admin reader uses a repeatable-read snapshot, the current plan/catalog and immutable report revision, rejects insufficient history, and returns unprojected accepted receipts, a `failed_events` subset for failed/skipped/missing worker jobs, retained lost-event day counts, current-authority excluded fact counts and `unverified` source coverage. It bounds relevant evidence from `from` through the received watermark and strictly before `to + conversion_window`; later project events cannot inflate this report's pending/lost counts or fact capacity. A failed job remains unprojected while its accepted raw object is retained; it becomes `lost` only after the retention transition confirms no completed job. A failed count never counts as a completed fact, and report quality remains partial. The reader forces `incomplete: true` until producer coverage and complete projection/rebuild are verified, so incomplete entrants remain unknown, rates are suppressed and quality cannot be exact. The day-level loss marker conservatively flags a range that intersects its occurrence day. This is not a public report interface or a complete correction/revision service; connected-space identity, source verification and broader metric effects remain gates. Facts cannot be submitted through a public endpoint. Callers may not silently omit unlinked, rejected, lost or unauthorized sources to claim complete coverage.

The internal portfolio projection uses the historical space-plan revision effective at acceptance, the origin project's accepted catalog revision and the covered event-entry revision. A separate forward-migrated fact table keys each effect by space, source project, immutable report revision and event. Identity remains framed in the origin project namespace, so matching opaque references from different projects are not joined. The internal repeatable-read reader requires current authority over every linked source and unchanged membership/catalog revisions; it returns one bounded result per source and a `catalog_gap` row with missing step keys for a source that cannot produce the defined funnel. It exposes no combined distinct subjects or aggregate rate. Every reported source remains `unverified`/partial with pending, failed, lost, excluded and erasure evidence until producer completeness, correction and identity authority are fully qualified. Detailed-history retention and individual erasure delete portfolio facts. Public nonempty space plans, report routes and capture remain disabled.

Each invocation accepts at most 10,000 compact facts, a cohort span at most ninety days, two through ten steps and a one-second through thirty-day conversion window. These are calculation ceilings, not paid entitlements or permission to retain more history. Larger datasets require qualified persistent aggregation rather than truncating the input. Breakdown values are capped at 32, with separate missing and ambiguous buckets. Exceeding bounds returns unavailable, never a partial table presented as complete.

The first eligible entry per subject in `[from,to)` starts the window. Events strictly before entry do not count. The deadline `entry + conversion_window` is inclusive; later steps can fall beyond `to`. The observation cutoff is inclusive and must be at least `to`; the received watermark must be at least the cutoff. A fact received after that watermark is absent from that report revision. One event cannot satisfy multiple steps, even when predicates overlap. Intermediate unrelated events are allowed.

Within an equal occurrence timestamp, chronological proof requires the same origin project, producer kind and non-null stream plus strictly increasing sequence numbers. Event UUID ordering is never causal proof. Unsequenced event IDs and producer stream IDs occupy separate internal key domains even when their UUID text is identical. Independent streams/projects and equal sequence numbers cannot order tied steps. Counts are confirmed progress only. Unresolved tied progress remains unknown; a later unambiguously completed path can establish completion. Earlier tied progress can still leave the earliest conversion time uncertain, so proving the count does not erase that quality reason. Conservative ambiguity is retained for incomplete paths rather than asserting a precise dropoff from uncertain ordering.

The output names the subject unit and exact scope/definition/calculation revisions. It returns aggregate entered/completed/open/expired/unknown populations, mature entries, confirmed step counts and explicit numerator/denominator pairs. Completed/open/expired/unknown partition entered subjects; mature overlaps those states and indicates a fully elapsed observation window. A zero denominator yields a null rate. Incomplete evidence suppresses rates and classifies unresolved subjects as unknown instead of expired. Sampling remains visible and is never extrapolated.

Quality is `partial` for incomplete evidence, sampling or unresolved order/entry-breakdown ambiguity; otherwise `provisional` for open entries or an unclosed correction window, and `exact` only for complete settled evidence. The correction window closes after `to + conversion_window + 48 hours` on the received watermark, including reports with no observed entries. Missing retained history or a definition starting after `from` returns unavailable rather than an exact zero. This calculation label does not prove that its caller loaded all evidence; durable loaders must establish that separately.

Breakdowns use the entry observation. Later plan/device/campaign changes cannot reclassify that entry. Null is distinct from a missing property. When the first timestamp has incomparable entry observations with different breakdowns, the subject goes into an explicit ambiguous bucket. Equal sequence entries are likewise unresolved. Raw subject references and event IDs never appear in output.

Conversion durations use confirmed completions only, in integer milliseconds, with explicit `basis: first_confirmed_path`. They measure the earliest path whose order the evidence proves, not an inferred earliest conversion when tied evidence could have completed sooner. Such timing uncertainty retains partial quality even when the eventual completion count is confirmed. Sum uses exact integer arithmetic; percentiles use the nearest-rank rule on the sorted duration population. Deterministic sorting makes equivalent inputs produce equal aggregate outputs without treating sorting as business order. Conflicting duplicates or stale scope/definition/subject-unit facts fail the whole calculation closed.

## Financial receipt calculations

`revenue-receipts.ts` implements the isolated calculation portion of FR-ANL-38/39 and AC-ANL-SEMANTIC-METRICS. It consumes bounded, protected receipt facts loaded under one currently authorized scope snapshot. It does not issue durable ACKs, authenticate a writer, claim quota, reconcile a payment provider or persist a receipt. Those remain service/storage gates.

Each fact retains the authenticated origin project, billing-source namespace, transport/operation/object references, authoritative occurrence and received timestamps, protected account key when actually known, and the money's currency-table version/exponent. A billing-source namespace is configured by the verified integration, not chosen from browser input or inferred from an account reference. Credentials may rotate without changing that namespace; different provider accounts must not share it. The loader resolves account keys under the authorized identity namespace. Equal provider object references from different origins/namespaces do not join.

Transport identity is `(origin project, event ID)`. Business-operation identity is `(origin project, billing-source namespace, operation kind, operation reference)`. Financial object identity is `(origin project, billing-source namespace, payment/refund kind, object reference)`. Thus different webhook types for the same successful payment count once even with distinct transport and operation IDs; two different purchases still count twice. Business comparisons use immutable financial content, original authoritative occurrence time and account association, excluding webhook delivery time and envelope IDs. A provider adapter must use the payment/refund's original committed timestamp on every notification. Conflicting evidence returns unavailable. UUID and protected-hash spellings are canonicalized before key/comparison use.

The report interval is `[from,to)`, at most ninety days; received timestamps are bounded by the supplied watermark. Payment/refund evidence before `from` may still be necessary for reconciliation. The caller must load each referenced payment and all of its preceding refunds through `to`, including a payment older than the report window. Missing predecessor receipts return `missing_payment`; exceeding retained history is not permission to count a refund or payment again. Refund arrival order is irrelevant, but its authoritative occurrence cannot precede the payment. Currency, exponent, currency-table interpretation and any supplied account association must match. Total refunds cannot exceed the original payment. Over-refunds, credits and chargebacks require separate explicitly typed future semantics; they cannot masquerade as a successful refund.

Gross is the sum of successful payments in the interval; refunded is the sum of successful refunds in the interval; net is gross minus refunded and can be negative when refunding older purchases. Average order value is gross divided by successful payment count, including zero-price completed orders. Paying accounts are the distinct known accounts with a positive successful payment in the interval, independent of subsequent refund status; positive payments with no known account have a separate coverage count. A free order does not create a paying account. Account counts across currency rows must not be summed as an overall distinct total.

Currency rows stay separate. Integer arithmetic uses `BigInt`, serialized as canonical decimal strings; no JSON numeric or floating-point money. Individual money is bounded by signed 64-bit positive range, input work by 10,000 facts and aggregates by 38 decimal digits. Exceeding a bound returns unavailable rather than truncation. A zero-order average is null. Exact fractional averages retain reduced numerator/denominator in minor units, with half-up display rounding after aggregation.

Successful output is `observed` as of the received watermark, or `partial` when known loss is declared. It is not a final accounting total: the durable reconciliation service must establish completeness and late-provider correction policy. Sampled financial evidence, insufficient history, stale revisions and conflicting input return unavailable. No raw account/event/operation identifiers appear in the aggregate. The current ISO table is pinned; a future table update must retain historical version lookup rather than reinterpret existing state or reject an in-horizon refund solely because its currency was retired.

## Recurring amount calculations

`financial-arithmetic.ts` implements exact monthly normalization over an already deduplicated, complete set of effective recurring rates. It is not yet the subscription state machine: effective-time selection, provider revision/reconciliation, account churn, movement classification and historical coverage are pending. A successful arithmetic result alone must not be presented as authoritative current MRR.

The provider-neutral convention is versioned as `recurring-revenue-1`: monthly amount/count; annual amount/(12 × count); daily amount × 365/(12 × count); weekly amount × 365/(84 × count). Daily/weekly rates use an explicitly fixed 365-day year so a seven-day rate equals an equivalent one-week rate. They are normalized run rates, not a calendar-month cash forecast or a claim to match every provider dashboard. Currency totals retain exact reduced fractions and round half up only after aggregation; never round each annual subscription before summing it.

Only positive active and past-due recurring rates contribute. Trials, pauses, canceled state and free plans contribute zero. The integration supplies the recurring committed amount after applicable recurring discounts/credits and excluding tax, one-time charges, usage-only charges and invoice proration. Payment/refund events never directly change MRR. A future scheduled cancellation remains active until its effective state change. Mixed item intervals/trials, finite discount changes and incomplete/unpaid provider states need explicit adapter/state representations before SDK signatures freeze; collapsing them into one arbitrary subscription amount is forbidden.

This separation follows the basic recurring-versus-receipt distinction and annual normalization documented in [Stripe Billing analytics](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/analytics). Stripe exposes configurable discount/activation definitions; DebugBundle must publish its own fixed versioned policy and must not imply identical dashboard totals under different settings.

## Calendar retention

`retention.ts` implements the bounded calculation portion of FR-ANL-37 and AC-ANL-SEMANTIC-METRICS. Facts carry their authorized scope/definition/subject revisions, protected subject key, immutable transport identity/content hash, occurrence/receipt times and compiled entry/return booleans. A caller must load all relevant authorized evidence from the stated tracking start, including entries before the requested cohort interval. The kernel cannot prove source completeness from rows alone.

The first entry observed since the later of retained-history availability and definition activation selects the cohort. A repeated entry never creates another cohort for that subject. A first entry before `[from,to)` excludes that subject from the requested entry population. The result explicitly says `first_observed_since_tracking_start`; it never claims first-ever acquisition. Definition, source, subject-unit or scope mismatches and conflicting transport duplicates fail closed. Input is limited to 10,000 facts and a ninety-day entry interval, with finite offsets from the shared definition schema. Output contains no subject references or individual journeys.

Calendar periods use the definition's IANA timezone and proleptic Gregorian dates. Days follow local dates, weeks start Monday and months start on the first; a day is not a fixed 24-hour duration. Daylight-saving changes, leap days and December/January transitions preserve calendar membership. The caller supplies the timezone-database version retained with the calculation snapshot. An unavailable runtime version returns unavailable rather than silently changing historical cohort boundaries; runtime tzdata changes require an explicit new calculation snapshot or qualified rebuild.

In `exact_period` mode, offset N asks whether the subject returned in calendar period N after its entry period. Only after that whole target period closes is the subject eligible for that cell's denominator. In `on_or_after` mode, N means N through the largest configured offset, inclusive; the largest offset is the explicit finite observation horizon. All such cells stay pending until that horizon closes, even if a return has already been observed. Events beyond the horizon cannot raise retention. There is no unbounded future-looking retention claim.

Each cell exposes eligible, retained, pending and unknown counts. Immature subjects are pending, never negative outcomes. Mature subjects with declared evidence loss are unknown and excluded from the rate. Summary cells sum the same eligible/retained populations across cohorts before forming the ratio; they do not average cohort percentages or include unobserved cells as zero. A zero denominator produces null. Sampled evidence is labeled partial without extrapolation, and known loss suppresses rates. Quality stays provisional while periods are immature or the 48-hour correction allowance after the observation cutoff has not elapsed on the received watermark; the empty case observes the same full-horizon rule.

Activation and loaders must enforce the effective retained-state budget in addition to the definition's syntactic offset limits. A full target calendar period can extend beyond `offset × nominal_days`: D90 may need 91 local calendar days including the entry day; weekly and monthly periods likewise need their actual boundary span, with correction state budgeted separately. No activation may silently extend the ninety-day detailed-state ceiling or claim a mature cell whose required evidence expired. The domain activation/reconciliation gates remain unfinished; the isolated kernel's caller-supplied availability is not proof that these gates ran.
